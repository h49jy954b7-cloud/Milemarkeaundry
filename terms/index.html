Effective December 1, 2026

These terms cover the machines at Mile Marker Laundry, operated by Leatherman Works LLC at 2201 Taft Hwy, Bakersfield, CA 93313.

Payment

The machines are app-only. You pay in the ShinePay app. There is no coin slot on the machine.

Refunds

If a cycle fails or stops before it should, you can request a refund in the ShinePay app. You can also report the problem or call (661) 360-6870, and we'll make it right.

Using the machines

Follow the instructions posted on the machine. Take your laundry out promptly when the cycle is done.

Don't wash items soaked in gasoline, oil, or chemicals, and don't use the machines to dye clothes.

Check pockets before you start. We aren't responsible for items that are lost, stolen, or left behind, or for damage caused by anything left in a pocket.

Prices can change. The price posted at the laundry is the one that applies.
