Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective December 1, 2026

Leatherman Works LLC, doing business as Mile Marker Laundry, runs this website. Our laundry is at 2201 Taft Hwy, Bakersfield, CA 93313. Questions about privacy go to support@milemarkerlaundry.com or (661) 360-6870.

The support form

If you report a machine problem, the form asks for your name, phone number, email address, machine number, and a short description of the issue. We use that information only to reply to you and to fix the problem you told us about.

The form is delivered by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). FormSubmit sends your message to us.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share it except with service providers we need to run the site and answer your report, such as FormSubmit and the email service that receives the message.

Washer and dryer payments happen entirely in the ShinePay app, under ShinePay's own privacy policy. We never see your card number or other card details.

The website itself

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, while serving the pages.

The location page includes a map embedded from Google Maps. When that map loads, Google may receive information under Google's own policies.

We don't track you across other websites. Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. Because we don't track you from site to site, there is no cross-site tracking for that signal to turn off.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that doesn't use cookies or track people across sites. It counts page views and shows general information such as the referring site, browser, device type, and country. It doesn't collect names or contact details, and we don't use it for advertising. GoatCounter's privacy policy explains how that works.

Seeing or deleting your information

Email support@milemarkerlaundry.com if you want to see the information from your report, or if you want us to delete it.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 13, please don't send us personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
